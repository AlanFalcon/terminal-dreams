What kind of spatial freedom?

Three models for making the world feel less like a corridor

A

Loose graph

Scenes connect in multiple directions. Go north, south, east, west. Backtracking is natural. You can get lost. The story still has a shape — there's a way forward — but the world doesn't feel like a hallway.

B

Zones with local freedom

Each act is a small cluster of 3–5 rooms you can wander freely. But moving between acts is still a one-way gate — crossing into Act 2 means Act 1 is behind you. Spatial within each act, linear across acts.

C

Richer exits, same spine

Keep the 10-scene narrative spine. But each scene gets real compass directions — north, south, east, west. Some lead to optional "texture rooms" (dead-ends with items or lore). The story still has a clear path but the world has depth.

All three keep the same number of scenes (~10). The difference is how much the player can deviate and whether they can go back.
