How do rooms connect within a zone?

5–7 rooms per zone. How should they be laid out?

A

Consistent grid

Rooms sit on a loose grid. If you go east from room A to room B, going west from B returns you to A. Directions are spatially honest. Players can build a mental map.

B

Loose graph

Rooms connect in ways that feel atmospheric, not geometric. Going north then south might not get you back. Directions are suggestive, not literal. Harder to map mentally — fits an uncanny or dreamlike tone.

C

Hub + spokes

Each zone has a central room. All others connect to it. You always return to the hub to reach a new room. Simple to navigate, impossible to get lost — but also less interesting to explore.
